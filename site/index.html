What is CrimeTime 2.0?

CrimeTime has helped New York prosecutors calculate permissible sentences since 1996. CrimeTime 2.0 is a research rebuild of that engine as a structured decision model: every offense in the Penal Law is classified, and the engine walks the statutory route — attempt and conspiracy reductions, hate-crime and terrorism bumps, predicate-offender status, armed-felony and domestic-violence exceptions — to the dispositions the court may impose.

What does CrimeTime do?

Choose a conviction offense and describe the defendant's record and the circumstances of the case. CrimeTime returns:

  • the final classification and the modifiers that produced it;
  • every authorized disposition — incarceration terms, post-release supervision, probation, conditional and unconditional discharge, fines;
  • exception paths that open alternative sentences;
  • mandatory surcharges, fees, and collateral requirements such as DNA and SORA;
  • a citable Sentencing Options Memorandum you can copy into your file.
Disclaimers

CrimeTime 2.0 is a research preview and is provided for training and educational purposes only. It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for reading the statutes, case law, and the court's own determination.
